Pro help

What to include in your email

The more we know, the faster we can help:

  • Your phone model and its Android or iOS version.
  • What you're mirroring to: Fire TV, Android TV or Google TV (and model), Windows or Mac.
  • How you connect: same Wi-Fi, no-router mode, or USB.
  • What you expected, what happened instead, and any message on screen. A photo or screenshot helps.
  • The email you signed in with, if your question is about your account or Pro.

Billing and refunds

Castiqq Pro is sold through the App Store and Google Play, so they handle payments, receipts, cancellations and refunds:

  • iPhone: open Settings › your name › Subscriptions to change or cancel, or request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.
  • Android: open the Google Play Store › your profile › Payments & subscriptions to change or cancel. Refunds follow Google Play's refund policy.
  • In Castiqq on your phone, Manage takes you to the right place. If Pro is missing after buying, open Get Pro and tap Restore purchases.

Delete your account

Email support@castiqq.com from the address you sign in with and ask us to delete your account. Deleting your account doesn't cancel an App Store or Google Play subscription, so cancel that there first. See the Privacy Policy for what we delete.
