Install the apps

Castiqq has an app for each screen. Install it on your phone and on whatever you want to watch on: your TV, your computer, or both.

Phone Android phone

  1. Open the Castiqq download link we sent you on the phone and download the app file (.apk).
  2. Open the downloaded file. If Android says installing from this source isn't allowed, tap Settings, turn on Allow from this source, then go back.
  3. Tap Install, then Open.

Works on Android 8 or newer. Sound needs Android 10 or newer.

Phone iPhone

  1. Open the Castiqq install link we sent you on the iPhone and follow the steps to install the app.
  2. Open Castiqq from your home screen.

Works on iOS 16.4 or newer.

TV Fire TV

  1. On the Fire TV home screen, search for Downloader and install it.
  2. Go to Settings › My Fire TV › About and click your device name 7 times until it says you're a developer.
  3. Go back to Settings › My Fire TV › Developer options › Install unknown apps and turn it on for Downloader.
  4. Open Downloader, type the Castiqq TV link we sent you, and select Go.
  5. When the download finishes, select Install, then Open. You'll find Castiqq TV under Your Apps & Channels from now on.

TV Android TV or Google TV

  1. Install Downloader from the Google Play Store on the TV.
  2. Open Downloader, type the Castiqq TV link we sent you, and select Go.
  3. If the TV blocks the install, select Settings in the message and allow Downloader to install unknown apps, then go back.
  4. Select Install, then Open.

Works on Android TV 8 or newer, including Google TV.

Computer Windows

  1. Download Castiqq-Setup.exe from the link we sent you and open it. (Prefer not to install? Use Castiqq-Portable.exe instead and just open it.)
  2. If Windows says Windows protected your PC, click More info, then Run anyway.
  3. When Windows Firewall asks, allow Castiqq on Private networks. Without this, phones can't find or reach your PC.

Computer Mac

  1. Download the .dmg from the link we sent you: arm64 for Macs with Apple chips (M1 and newer), x64 for Intel Macs.
  2. Open it and drag Castiqq into Applications.
  3. Open Castiqq. If macOS won't open it, go to System Settings › Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway.
  4. When asked, allow Local Network access so phones can find your Mac.

Sign in

You need a free account to use Castiqq. Sign in on your phone first, then use your phone to sign in your computer and your TV.

Phone On your phone

  1. PhoneOpen Castiqq and tap Sign in.
  2. PhoneSign in, or create an account if you don't have one yet. You come back to Castiqq when you're done.
  3. PhoneYour email shows at the top, with Free or Pro under it. Sign out is next to it.

Computer On your computer

  1. ComputerOpen Castiqq. It shows Sign in to Castiqq, with a QR code on the right.
  2. PhoneIn Castiqq on your signed-in phone, tap Scan the QR code and point the camera at the code on the computer.
  3. ComputerThe computer signs in to the same account and opens on Receive. Your email shows at the top. You only do this once.

TV On your TV

  1. TVOpen Castiqq TV. It shows Sign in to Castiqq, with a QR code on the right.
  2. PhoneIn Castiqq on your signed-in phone, tap Scan the QR code and scan the code on the TV.
  3. TVThe TV signs in and shows its mirroring code and PIN. You only do this once.
Code expired? The code changes after a short while. Scan the one on the screen now. Phone and computer need to be on the same Wi-Fi.

Mirror your phone to your TV

The everyday way: your phone and TV on the same Wi-Fi.

You'll need: Castiqq on the phone and on the TV, both on the same Wi-Fi network.

  1. TVOpen Castiqq TV. It shows its name, a 4-digit PIN and its address on the left, and a QR code on the right.
  2. PhoneOpen Castiqq. Then connect in one of two ways:

    Scan the code (quickest)

    1. Tap Scan the QR code.
    2. Point the camera at the code on the TV. The TV and its PIN are filled in for you.

    Pick from the list

    1. Tap your TV under TVs on this network.
    2. Type the PIN shown on the TV under PIN shown on the TV.
  3. PhoneTap Mirror to and your TV's name.
  4. PhoneAllow the screen capture:
    • Android: tap Start now. If you're asked what to share, choose Entire screen.
    • iPhone: in the screen recording sheet, make sure Castiqq is selected and tap Start Broadcast.
  5. TVYour phone's screen and sound now play on the TV.
Next time is faster. Your phone remembers the TV under Your devices: just tap it. See Your devices. TV not in the list? Type the address shown on the TV under Or enter the address shown on the TV.

Mirror your phone to your computer

Your Windows PC or Mac works just like a TV.

You'll need: Castiqq on the phone and on the computer, both on the same Wi-Fi network.

  1. ComputerOpen Castiqq. It starts on Receive, laid out like the TV: the computer's name, PIN and address on the left, and a QR code on the right. In a narrow window the code moves above them.
  2. PhoneOpen Castiqq and tap Scan the QR code, then point the camera at the code on the computer. Or pick the computer from the list and type its PIN.
  3. PhoneTap Mirror to and the computer's name, then allow the screen capture as above.
  4. ComputerYour phone appears in the window. Double-click it or press F for full screen.

Mirror without a Wi-Fi router

In a hotel, dorm, or anywhere without Wi-Fi you can use. The TV or a Windows PC makes its own small network and your phone joins it.

TV To a TV

  1. TVIf the TV is connected to Wi-Fi, disconnect it first in the TV's network settings.
  2. TVIn Castiqq TV, select No Wi-Fi router? Start no-router mode. Allow the permission if the TV asks. The TV now shows its own Wi-Fi name and password.
  3. PhoneTap Scan the QR code and scan the code. Your phone joins the TV's network and connects in one go.
    • Or tap More ways to connect and type the Wi-Fi name and password under No router? Join the TV's network and tap Join network.
  4. PhoneTap Mirror to and your TV's name.
  5. TVWhen you're done, select Stop no-router mode.

Computer To a Windows PC

  1. ComputerOn the Receive screen, click Start under No router. The QR code now includes the PC's network name and password.
  2. PhoneScan the code. The phone joins the PC's network and connects.
  3. PhoneTap Mirror to and the PC's name.
Phone joins but can't connect? Windows Firewall is blocking the new network. In Windows Security › Firewall & network protection › Allow an app through firewall, tick Public for Castiqq.

Mirror an Android phone over USB

A cable gives the steadiest picture when Wi-Fi is weak. This works from an Android phone to a computer.

You'll need: a USB cable, and USB debugging turned on (one-time setup below). The computer also needs Android's adb tool installed.

One-time setup: turn on USB debugging

  1. PhoneGo to Settings › About phone and tap Build number 7 times until it says you're a developer. (On Samsung: Settings › About phone › Software information.)
  2. PhoneGo to Settings › Developer options and turn on USB debugging.

Mirror

  1. ComputerOpen Castiqq on the Receive screen and plug in the phone.
  2. PhoneIf asked to Allow USB debugging, tap Allow. Tick Always allow from this computer to skip this next time.
  3. ComputerClick Mirror over USB next to your phone under USB (Android).
  4. PhoneCastiqq opens by itself with the computer already picked. Just allow the screen capture.
No adb on the computer? Turn on USB tethering on the phone instead (Settings › Network › Hotspot & tethering), then scan the computer's QR code as usual. The connection runs over the cable.

Mirror your phone to a web browser

No Castiqq app on the other screen? Any web browser works: a Samsung or LG TV's browser, a laptop, a work computer.

You'll need: an Android phone, and the other device on the same Wi-Fi.

  1. PhoneOpen Castiqq and tap Mirror to a web browser, then allow the screen capture.
  2. PhoneThe phone shows an address like http://192.168.1.30:8080, a QR code and a PIN.
  3. TVor ComputerOpen the web browser, type the address, then type the PIN on the page.
  4. TVor ComputerYour phone's screen and sound play in the browser. If you reload the page, click once to turn the sound back on.
To stop, tap Stop mirroring on the phone, or Disconnect on the page.

Share your computer's screen

Send a Windows PC's or Mac's screen, or just one window, to a TV or to another computer.

You'll need: Castiqq on both devices, on the same Wi-Fi network. On a Mac, first allow Castiqq in System Settings › Privacy & Security › Screen & System Audio Recording, then reopen it.

  1. TVor ComputerOn the screen you want to watch on, open Castiqq TV, or Castiqq on its Receive screen.
  2. ComputerOn the computer you're sharing from, click Send this screen.
  3. ComputerUnder What to share, pick your whole screen or a window.
  4. ComputerUnder Send to, pick the TV or computer. Not listed? Type its address. Click Refresh to look again.
  5. ComputerChoose Quality and whether to Send sound, then click Start mirroring.
  6. ComputerThe first time, type the PIN shown on the other screen and click Connect.
Sound: Windows sends sound. A Mac usually sends the picture only. You can change Quality while mirroring.

To a TV or computer without Castiqq

  1. ComputerOn Send this screen, pick Web browser at the top of the Send to list and click Start mirroring.
  2. ComputerCastiqq shows an address like 192.168.1.20:8080 and a PIN.
  3. TVor ComputerOn the other device, open its web browser, go to that address and type the PIN. Your screen appears.

Use a tablet as a second monitor

Turn an Android tablet (or a TV, or another computer) into an extra screen for your PC or Mac. Drag windows onto it like any monitor.

You'll need: Castiqq TV on the tablet (the same app as on TVs; it installs on tablets too), and both on the same Wi-Fi. A Mac needs macOS 11 or newer.

  1. TVOpen Castiqq TV on the tablet.
  2. ComputerClick Send this screen and set Send as to An extra screen.
  3. ComputerWindows only, the first time: click Install the driver and allow the admin prompt. This adds the free Virtual Display Driver to Windows.
  4. ComputerPick the tablet and click Start extending.
  5. TVThe tablet becomes a new screen to the right of your others. To move it, use your computer's display settings.
Good to know: touching the tablet doesn't control the computer yet, so use your mouse and keyboard. Stopping removes the extra screen. If Windows says the driver stopped working, click Restart the driver.

Mirror to a TV without the Castiqq app

Chromecasts, Google TVs and most smart TVs that play videos from your network (many Samsung, LG, Sony and Philips sets) work without installing anything on them.

You'll need: an Android phone, and the TV on the same Wi-Fi.

  1. PhoneOpen Castiqq. Your TV shows up under TVs without the Castiqq app.
  2. PhoneTap the TV and allow the screen capture.
  3. TVYour phone's screen and sound play on the TV, up to 1080p.
  4. PhoneTo stop, tap Stop mirroring on the phone, or stop it with the TV's remote.
A few seconds behind: these TVs buffer the stream, so the picture runs a little behind your phone. That's fine for videos and photos. For games, use the Castiqq TV app.

Two phones on one TV

While one phone is mirroring to an Android TV, a second phone can join. The TV shows both side by side.

  1. PhoneOn the second phone, open Castiqq and pick the TV as usual.
  2. TVA phone that has mirrored to this TV before joins straight away. A new one needs the PIN: press OK on the remote and the TV shows it for a few seconds.
  3. TVYou hear one phone at a time. Press left or right on the remote to choose which.
  4. TVBack stops the phone you hear, and the other one fills the screen again.
Good to know: with two phones, each sends at most 1080p so the TV keeps up. A TV that can only play one video at a time says it is busy instead.

Use your phone as a webcam

Use an Android phone's camera as the webcam in Zoom, Google Meet, Teams or the Camera app on a Windows PC.

You'll need: an Android phone and a Windows PC running Castiqq, on the same Wi-Fi. On Windows 10 you also need OBS Studio (free), installed once. Windows 11 needs nothing extra.

  1. PhoneOpen Castiqq and go to the Webcam tab. Pick Front camera or Back camera.
  2. PhonePick your PC and tap Send camera. Stand the phone on its side, like a webcam.
  3. ComputerCastiqq turns on Use as webcam by itself and shows the camera's name to pick.
  4. ComputerWindows 11, the first time: allow the permission prompt. This adds Castiqq Camera to Windows, once per computer.
  5. ComputerIn Zoom, Meet, Teams or the Camera app, choose the camera:
    • Windows 11: Castiqq Camera.
    • Windows 10: OBS Virtual Camera. OBS doesn't need to be open, but its own virtual camera must be off.
Mirrored screen as a webcam: while a phone's screen shows on the PC, click Use as webcam in the bar over the picture. On a Mac, the camera shows in the window but can't be used as a webcam yet.

Make videos with Studio

Studio turns the Castiqq app on your computer into a small video studio for YouTube: switch between up to four phones, add a facecam, record, save replays and go live.

You'll need: Castiqq on a Windows PC or Mac, and a phone already mirroring to it. For NDI, also NDI Tools (free), installed once.

Open Studio and add phones

  1. ComputerWhile a phone is showing, click Studio in the bar over the picture.
  2. PhoneTo add more phones (up to four in all), pick this computer on each one and type the PIN that Studio shows.
  3. PhoneFor a facecam, send a phone's Front camera or Back camera. It appears in a corner of the picture.
  4. ComputerPick a layout: Normal, Facecam, or Shorts (9:16) for vertical videos. Where the picture's shape leaves room, a blurred copy of it fills the frame.
  5. ComputerPress 1 to 4 to put a phone on screen. Hold Ctrl+Alt (⌘+Option on a Mac) and the keys work from any app, so your game or OBS can stay in front.

Record, replays and chapters

  1. ComputerClick Record (or press R). The video is saved as an MP4, in 1080p at 30 fps with the phone's sound, to your Videos folder.
  2. ComputerTick Mic on its own track to also save your microphone as a separate M4A file, so you can mix your voice while editing.
  3. ComputerPress Marker (or M) at each new topic. The chapters are saved next to the video in a text file, ready to paste into the YouTube description.
  4. ComputerMissed a great moment? Click Save replay (or press P) to save the last 30 seconds, even when you weren't recording.

Go live on YouTube

  1. ComputerIn YouTube Studio, choose Go live › Stream and copy the stream key.
  2. ComputerIn Castiqq Studio, click Go live, paste the key and click Start streaming. The YouTube server is already filled in, and the key is stored encrypted on your computer.

Send to OBS or vMix with NDI

  1. ComputerInstall NDI Tools (and the NDI plugin if you use OBS).
  2. ComputerTurn on NDI in Studio. Each phone shows up as its own NDI source in OBS, vMix and other NDI apps on your network.
Good to know: while Studio is open, Use as webcam sends Studio's picture, layout and facecam included. See Phone as a webcam.

Clean screen, Show taps and teleprompter

Tools on an Android phone that make recordings and tutorials look tidier.

  1. PhoneClean screen for videos: turn it on in Castiqq's Studio tab before you mirror. The status bar is left out of the picture and notifications are hidden.
  2. PhoneShow taps: shows a dot wherever your finger touches, so viewers can follow along. Tap the row in Castiqq to open Developer options and turn it on. If the phone is plugged into your computer, you can also tick Show taps next to it in the desktop app's USB list.
  3. PhoneTeleprompter: paste your script ahead of time from the Studio tab. While sending the camera, tap Teleprompter and Start. The text scrolls near the top of the screen, close to the front camera, so you look into the lens. Tap the text to pause.

Sound, quality and game mode

Set these on your phone's Settings tab before you tap Mirror to.

  1. PhoneSend audio: turn on to hear your phone through the TV or computer. Some apps don't allow their sound to be captured, and those stay silent.
  2. PhoneHide notifications on the TV (Android): keeps messages off the big screen. The first time, Castiqq asks for Do Not Disturb access. Allow it, come back, and tap Mirror to again.
  3. PhoneQuality: pick how sharp the picture is.
  4. PhoneGame mode: shows each picture as soon as it arrives, for the least delay. On weak Wi-Fi the picture skips instead of falling behind. An Android TV also switches to its game picture mode. On a computer, tick Game mode in the Send this screen options.
QualityPictureBest for
Max (Pro)Your phone's full screen resolution, up to 4K, 60 fpsThe sharpest picture on a 4K TV or big monitor, with fast 5 GHz Wi-Fi or USB
High (Pro)1080p, 60 fpsVideos and games on good Wi-Fi or USB
Medium720p, 30 fpsMost home Wi-Fi
Low540p, 30 fpsBusy or weak Wi-Fi
Change it any time. On Android, the quality buttons stay on the mirroring screen, so you can switch while mirroring.
About Max: it never sends more than your phone's screen has, so a 1080p phone gets about the same picture as High. If the phone or TV can't handle the full size, Max uses the largest size they support.

Control the TV's volume from your phone

While you're mirroring, your phone's volume buttons turn the TV or computer up and down, even from another app or with the screen off.

  1. PhoneStart mirroring as usual.
  2. PhonePress volume up or down. The TV's or computer's volume changes, and your phone shows its level.
  3. PhoneIf a video app keeps the buttons for itself, Castiqq shows a small volume bar of its own. Allow Display over other apps for Castiqq so it can show over the app (tap appear on top on the mirroring screen).
Fire TV and other HDMI sticks: the stick passes the volume to your TV over the HDMI cable using HDMI-CEC. Turn it on in your TV's settings (it may be called Anynet+, SimpLink, Bravia Sync or similar). The phone then shows up and down instead of a level, because the TV's level can't be read.

Works with Android TV and Google TV, Fire TV, Windows, Mac and the web browser page. Your phone's own volume goes back to normal when you stop.

Locking the phone, and your devices

Phone If you lock the phone

On newer Android phones, locking stops the screen capture. Castiqq keeps the TV connected for up to 10 minutes and the TV or computer shows a clock.

  1. PhoneUnlock the phone and tap the Castiqq notification, or open the app.
  2. PhoneTap Resume mirroring and allow the capture again.
Resume by itself: on the mirroring screen, tap appear on top and turn on Display over other apps for Castiqq. Then unlocking brings mirroring back on its own.

Phone Your devices

  1. PhoneEvery TV and computer you've mirrored to is listed under Your devices. Tap one marked Nearby · tap to mirror to connect without a PIN or code.
  2. PhoneTurn on Connect automatically when the app opens to connect to the last one by itself.
  3. PhoneTap Forget to remove a device. You'll need its PIN or QR code again next time.

Full screen and stopping

  1. ComputerDouble-click the picture or press F for full screen. Press Esc to leave it.
  2. ComputerShowing a phone? Click Phone view to shape the window like the phone. It turns when the phone does.
  3. PhoneTo stop, tap Stop mirroring in Castiqq or in its notification.
  4. TVOr press Back on the remote. On a computer, click Stop.
  5. ComputerWhile something is showing, the computer hides the Receive and Send this screen tabs. They come back when mirroring stops.

Castiqq Pro

Everything works for free. Pro adds Max quality (your phone's full screen resolution, up to 4K) and High quality (1080p), both at 60 fps, and removes the Get Pro banner shown next to the picture on the TV and computer.

  1. PhoneSign in, then tap Get Pro at the top of the Settings tab. Tapping Max or High under Quality also opens it.
  2. PhonePick Monthly ($9.99), Yearly ($79.99) or Lifetime ($99.99) and confirm with the App Store or Google Play.
  3. PhoneNew to Pro? Monthly and Yearly start with a 14-day free trial: tap Start 14-day free trial. You're charged on day 15 unless you cancel first in Manage.
  4. TVor ComputerNothing to do. They see that you have Pro when your phone connects.
New phone, or reinstalled? Sign in with the same account. If Pro doesn't show, open Get Pro and tap Restore purchases. To change or cancel a plan, tap Manage.

Troubleshooting

The computer only shows a sign-in code

It needs to be signed in first. On your phone, sign in to Castiqq, tap Scan the QR code and scan the code on the computer. Both need to be on the same Wi-Fi. See Sign in.

The TV only shows a sign-in code

Sign it in once from your phone: tap Scan the QR code and scan the code on the TV. See Sign in.

The volume buttons change my phone, not the TV

They only work the TV while mirroring is on. With a Fire TV or other stick, turn on HDMI-CEC on the TV. See Volume buttons.

Mirroring paused when I locked my phone

Unlock it and tap Resume mirroring. To skip this, allow Display over other apps. See Locking the phone.

My TV or computer isn't in the list

Check both are on the same Wi-Fi. Guest networks and some routers hide devices from each other. Scan the QR code or type the address shown on the TV or computer instead.

The computer is listed but my phone can't connect

Windows: allow Castiqq through Windows Firewall on Private networks, or on Public for no-router mode. Mac: allow Local Network in System Settings › Privacy & Security.

No sound on the TV

Make sure Send audio is on. Android needs version 10 or newer, and some apps don't allow their sound to be captured.

The picture is blocky or stutters

Max needs a strong connection. Pick High, Medium or Low, move closer to the router, or use USB from an Android phone to a computer. After you pause a video, the picture can look soft for a moment. It sharpens again within a few seconds.

"This TV cannot host a hotspot while on Wi-Fi"

Disconnect the TV from Wi-Fi in its network settings, then start no-router mode again.

Mirror over USB doesn't appear

Check USB debugging is on, tap Allow on the phone's prompt, and try unplugging and plugging the cable in again. Or use USB tethering and scan the code.

Zoom or Teams doesn't list Castiqq Camera

That camera is on Windows 11 only. Allow the permission prompt the first time, then restart the video app so it looks for cameras again. On Windows 10, or if Windows 11 can't add it, install OBS Studio and pick OBS Virtual Camera. See Phone as a webcam.

My TV is under "TVs without the Castiqq app" but nothing plays

Check the TV is on and on the same Wi-Fi, and give it a few seconds to start: these TVs buffer before they play. If it still doesn't, install Castiqq TV or use the TV's web browser.

I see a Get Pro banner after buying Pro

Make sure you're signed in on the phone with the account you bought Pro on, then open Get Pro and tap Restore purchases. Reconnect to the TV or computer.
