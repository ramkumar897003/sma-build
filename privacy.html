Privacy Policy

What we collect, what we don't, and the choices you have.

Effective October 25, 2026

This policy explains what information the Castiqq apps (for phones, TVs, Windows and Mac) and this website handle, and why. Castiqq is provided by Castiqq Labs LLC ("we", "us"). Questions: support@castiqq.com.

The short version

  • What you mirror (your screen and sound) goes straight from your device to your TV or computer over your local network or a USB cable. It never passes through our servers, and we don't record or store it.
  • If you sign in, we keep your account details (such as your email address) so you can use Castiqq Pro on your devices.
  • Payments are handled by Apple or Google. We never see your card details.
  • The apps have no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

You can use Castiqq without signing in when the app version you have doesn't require it. Then none of the account or purchase details above are collected.

What we don't collect

  • Your screen, sound, photos, videos or anything else you mirror.
  • Your contacts, messages, location or browsing.
  • Advertising IDs or analytics about how you use the apps.

Information kept only on your devices

To make reconnecting quick, the apps store some information on your own devices, and it isn't sent to us:

  • The phone remembers TVs and computers you've paired with and your settings (quality, sound, hidden notifications).
  • TVs and computers remember the phones and computers that paired with them, and show their device names while mirroring.
  • Devices on your local network can see a Castiqq TV or computer's name so you can find it in the app.

Permissions the apps ask for

  • Screen capture or screen recording: to mirror your screen. Only while you're mirroring.
  • Microphone or audio capture (Android): to send your phone's sound. Castiqq doesn't record your voice. It's only used to capture the sound playing on the phone.
  • Camera: to scan the QR code on a TV or computer. Images aren't saved or sent.
  • Notifications and Do Not Disturb (Android): to show the mirroring notification, and to hide your notifications on the TV if you turn that on.
  • Local network and Wi-Fi: to find and connect to your TVs and computers, and to join a TV's or PC's own network in no-router mode.
  • USB debugging (Android, optional): only if you choose to mirror to a computer over a cable.

Service providers

We use these companies to run sign-in and subscriptions. They process information on our behalf under their own privacy policies:

  • Clerk, for accounts and sign-in.
  • RevenueCat, for subscriptions.
  • Apple and Google, for app stores and payments, and for sign-in if you choose them.

We don't sell your information or share it for advertising.

How long we keep it

We keep your account and purchase details while you have an account. If you ask us to delete your account, we delete your account details and ask our providers to delete theirs within 30 days. Apple and Google keep their own purchase records.

Your choices and rights

  • Sign out at any time in the phone app.
  • Email support@castiqq.com to see, correct or delete your account details.
  • Depending on where you live, you may have more rights, such as objecting to processing or complaining to your data protection authority.

Security

Sign-in and purchases use our providers' encrypted connections. Mirroring only connects to TVs and computers you pair with a PIN or QR code. Your mirrored content travels over your own local network or cable, so use a network you trust.

Children

Castiqq isn't aimed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information. If you think a child has given us information, contact us and we'll delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top and, for important changes, tell you in the app or by email.
